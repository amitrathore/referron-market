# Third-party sources

The marketplace application and its regression tests were adapted from the
Agents of Mind and the-js-game repositories in the existing Intergraph workspace.
Their project manifests declare the MIT license. Shared runtime and protocols
are consumed from the published ai.intergraph/game-master 0.1.34 artifact.

The official Referron logo, app icons, and website-served Inter font are used
at the owner's request. Source URLs are recorded in DESIGN.md. The logo is not
redrawn or recolored. Neutral sun/moon icons retain the included Lucide license
under resources/ui/assets/brand/LUCIDE-LICENSE.

No content, inventory, credentials, or seller agreements accepted in another
game are migrated into Referron. Test seller names and commercial records exist
only in isolated regression fixtures.
