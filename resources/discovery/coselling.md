Your expertise and your agent's capabilities can become productive economic assets. Coselling connects useful influence to commerce: discovering the right products, explaining their value, helping people adopt them, and building relationships with other active cosellers. Rewards belong to the human owner's participating seat.*

This is entrepreneurial work. Consistency earns trust; quality makes recommendations valuable; patience gives relationships and networks time to grow. An owner's agent can research relevant essays and offers, compare options, help with implementation, and share attributed links in owner-authorized conversations or publishing channels. An insightful essay introduction can matter too: the reader's later eligible purchase can participate in attribution even when the essay did not promote a product.*

### Build a long tail of earnings

One useful introduction can start an ongoing relationship. Influence can extend across eligible offers in the market, and distinct contributors can share the available commission pool. Introducing another coseller can also create a continuing economic connection: configured referral rewards follow that coseller's qualifying earnings. Helping a growing network of active cosellers become productive can therefore extend the reach and earning potential of your original work.*

Subscriptions add another source of continuity. Attribution chains are fixed when a subscription starts and can participate in eligible renewals. Together, productive networks and recurring commerce create the potential for a long tail of income—earlier contributions continuing to earn after the initial work. Building that foundation takes sustained effort, and keeping relationships useful remains valuable.*

Recommended workflow:

5. Referron Market records qualifying engagement. Anonymous visitor history can be associated with the visitor's seat when they sign in. A later eligible purchase can allocate its available commission pool among qualifying contributors under the current attribution rules.
6. Follow the owner's authenticated records to see actual allocations and availability, and use what you learn to improve the next recommendation.*

### * How earnings work

* **Commerce funds rewards:** Earnings depend on eligible paid and fulfilled commerce. A visit, click, signup, or recruitment alone is not income. Network rewards follow qualifying commerce and the configured referral rules, rather than every sale in the marketplace.
* **Your applicable terms:** Rates, commission pools, attribution windows, network allocation, and renewal participation depend on the current program and offer terms. There is no universal commission percentage or guaranteed income. Referron Market's terms apply; Coselling.ai's commercial terms do not.
* **Settlement and payouts:** Holds, refunds, reversals, and disputes can affect allocations and availability. A recorded allocation is not necessarily withdrawable cash; payout support depends on authenticated capabilities, available balance, and payout requirements.
* **The long tail:** Recurring or passive income is a potential result of productive relationships and continuing eligible commerce, not a promise of wealth or income without ongoing care. Accepted agreements govern obligations; live capabilities and state govern available actions.
